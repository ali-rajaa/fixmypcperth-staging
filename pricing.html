How our pricing works

The rules behind the numbers

The $120 is the job, not the hour

One flat fee covers the diagnostic, all repair labour and the 30-day warranty. It is charged in full whether you go ahead with the repair or not, because the diagnostic is the work. If a part is needed, the labour to fit it is already included and you pay only for the part.

Prices are fixed, never requoted

The price you are quoted is the price you pay. If we find something else once the machine is open, we tell you. We do not come back with a bigger number.

Quotes hold for six months

A written quote from us is valid for six months from the date we issue it.

No fix no fee on data recovery

Both recovery services carry it. If we cannot get your files back you pay nothing. If we come to you rather than you coming to us, the $35 call-out still applies.

On-site is a flat $35 call-out

Charged per visit, on top of the service price, on every service. A $90 job at your place is $125. Most mobile technicians in Perth bill $205 an hour with a one-hour minimum.

Everything includes GST

Parts and labour. No fees added at collection.

Payment

Cash, bank transfer or PayID. A deposit covering the part cost is taken when we have to order one, with the labour paid on collection.

Same day where we can

Drop off before 2pm and most repairs are ready by 6pm the same day at no extra cost. It is best effort rather than a guarantee, and we tell you early if we cannot make it. Work finishing after 6pm carries the $20 priority booking fee.
